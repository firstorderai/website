How to setup 2FA for Restream.io account?

To setup 2FA for your Restream.io account with our Authenticator App, you need to follow the next steps:

  1. Log in to your account on the Restream.io website, then navigate to the "Settings" section.

  2. Click on the "Account" tab.

  3. Find and click "Enable two-factor authentication".

  4. A QR code containing the secret key will be displayed. Next, you need to use our Authenticator App to scan this QR code. Please keep this popup open to complete the setup.

  5. Open our Authenticator App, click the + button at the bottom right of the screen, then hold your device near the computer screen to scan the QR code.

  6. After successfully scanning, a 6-digit code will be shown at the bottom of the screen. Please note that this code refreshes every 30 seconds.

  7. Return to the Restream.io webpage, and enter the 6-digit code displayed in step 6.

  8. Type your password in the provided field.

  9. Click "Submit".

  10. Remember to click "Save" in the Authenticator App to save your account, or you may lose access to your account!

  11. Now, you have successfully setup 2FA for your Restream.io account. From now on, when you attempt to sign in to your Restream.io account, you will need to use our Authenticator App.
