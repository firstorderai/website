How to setup 2FA for Zapier account?

To setup 2FA for your Zapier account with our Authenticator App, you need to follow the next steps:

  1. Log in to your account on the Zapier website, then click on your profile icon in the top right corner and select "Settings".

  2. In the left sidebar under the "My account" section, select "Security and data".

  3. In the "Security" section, select "Two-Factor Authentication". You will be redirected to a new page.

  4. Click "Setup Two-Factor Authentication". You'll be redirected to the 2FA setup page.

  5. In the "Get an Authentication App" section, click "Next Step".

  6. In this step, a QR code containing the secret key is displayed. Next, you need to use our Authenticator App to scan this QR code. Please keep this popup open to complete the setup.

  7. Open our Authenticator App, click the + button at the bottom right of the screen, then hold your device near the computer screen to scan the QR code.

  8. After successfully scanning, a 6-digit code will be shown at the bottom of the screen. Please note that this code refreshes every 30 seconds.

  9. Return to the Zapier webpage, enter the 6-digit code displayed in step 8, and then click "Next Step".

  10. Remember to click "Save" in the Authenticator App to save your account, or you may lose access to your account!

  11. Save your recovery codes in a secure place. You can also add them to our Authenticator App for easy access.

  12. Now, you have successfully setup 2FA for your Zapier account. From now on, when you attempt to sign in to your Zapier account, you will need to use our Authenticator App.
