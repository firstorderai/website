How to setup 2FA for AppFolio account?

To setup 2FA for your AppFolio account with our Authenticator App, you need to follow the next steps:

  1. Log in to your AppFolio account and navigate to the Account Profile tab.

  2. In the Two Factor Authentication section, check Enable Two Factor Authentication, then click Save.

  3. A QR code containing the secret key will be displayed. Keep this page open to complete the setup.

  4. Open our Authenticator App, click the + button at the bottom right of the screen, then hold your device near the computer screen to scan the QR code.

  5. After successfully scanning, a 6-digit code will be shown at the bottom of the screen. Please note that this code refreshes every 30 seconds.

  6. Return to the AppFolio webpage, enter the 6-digit code generated by the Authenticator App, then click Activate.

  7. Remember to click "Save" in the Authenticator App to save your account, or you may lose access to your account!

  8. Now, you have successfully setup 2FA for your AppFolio account. From now on, when you attempt to sign in to your AppFolio account, you will need to use our Authenticator App.
