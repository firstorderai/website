How to setup 2FA for SeatGeek account?

To setup 2FA for your SeatGeek account with our Authenticator App, you need to follow the next steps:

  1. Log in to your account on the SeatGeek website, then navigate to the Security tab in your account settings: seatgeek.com.

  2. Select Authentication App as your 2FA method.

  3. A QR code containing the secret key will be displayed. Please keep this page open to complete the setup.

  4. Open our Authenticator App, click the + button at the bottom right of the screen, then hold your device near the computer screen to scan the QR code.

  5. After successfully scanning, a 6-digit code will be shown at the bottom of the screen. Please note that this code refreshes every 30 seconds.

  6. Return to the SeatGeek webpage, and enter the 6-digit code displayed in step 5.

  7. Remember to click "Save" in the Authenticator App to save your account, or you may lose access to your account!

  8. Now, you have successfully setup 2FA for your SeatGeek account. From now on, when you attempt to sign in to your SeatGeek account, you will need to use our Authenticator App.
