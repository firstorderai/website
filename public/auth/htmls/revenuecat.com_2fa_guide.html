How to setup 2FA for RevenueCat account?

To setup 2FA for your RevenueCat account with our Authenticator App, you need to follow the next steps:

  1. Log in to your RevenueCat dashboard and navigate to Account > Security settings.

  2. Click Set up under Two-factor Authentication to begin the setup process.

  3. You'll be prompted to re-enter your password. Once re-authenticated, you'll be presented with a QR code that you should scan with an Authenticator App.

  4. Open our Authenticator App, click the + button at the bottom right of the screen, then hold your device near the computer screen to scan the QR code.

  5. After successfully scanning, a 6-digit code will be shown at the bottom of the screen. Please note that this code refreshes every 30 seconds.

  6. Return to the RevenueCat dashboard, enter the 6-digit code displayed in step 5, and then click Enable.

  7. Remember to click "Save" in the Authenticator App to save your account, or you may lose access to your account!
