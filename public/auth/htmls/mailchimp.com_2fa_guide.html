How to setup 2FA for MailChimp account?

To setup 2FA for your MailChimp account with our Authenticator App, you need to follow the next steps:

  1. Log in to your account on the MailChimp website, then click your profile icon in the bottom left corner and select "Account".

  2. Click "Settings" in the left-hand menu.

  3. Select "Two-factor authentication".

  4. In the 2-factor authentication section, select the "Authenticator app" radio button and click "Next".

  5. A QR code containing the secret key will be displayed. Next, you need to use our Authenticator App to scan this QR code. Please keep this popup open to complete the setup.

  6. Open our Authenticator App, click the + button at the bottom right of the screen, then hold your device near the computer screen to scan the QR code.

  7. After successfully scanning, a 6-digit code will be shown at the bottom of the screen. Please note that this code refreshes every 30 seconds.

  8. Return to the MailChimp webpage, enter the 6-digit code displayed in step 7, and then click "Enable".

  9. Save the backup code displayed on the confirmation page offline or on a separate device. You'll need the backup code to log in to MailChimp if you ever lose, restore, or damage your device.

  10. Remember to click "Save" in the Authenticator App to save your account, or you may lose access to your account!

  11. Click "Close" to finish the setup.

  12. Now, you have successfully setup 2FA for your MailChimp account. From now on, when you attempt to sign in to your MailChimp account, you will need to use our Authenticator App.
