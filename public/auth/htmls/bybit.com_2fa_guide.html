How to setup 2FA for Bybit account?

To setup 2FA for your Bybit account with our Authenticator App, you need to follow the next steps:

  1. Log in to your account on the Bybit website, then navigate to your account's security settings page. Click on the "Settings" button as shown below.

  2. Click on "Send Verification Code". A verification code will be sent to either your registered email address or registered mobile number. Enter the code into the empty boxes and click "Confirm".

  3. A pop-up window showing a QR code will appear.

  4. Open our Authenticator App, click the + button at the bottom right of the screen, then hold your device near the computer screen to scan the QR code.

  5. After successfully scanning, a 6-digit code will be shown at the bottom of the screen. Please note that this code refreshes every 30 seconds.

  6. Return to the Bybit webpage, enter the 6-digit code generated in the Authenticator App, and then click "Confirm".

  7. Remember to click "Save" in the Authenticator App to save your account, or you may lose access to your account!

You have successfully bound your Authenticator App to your Bybit account.
