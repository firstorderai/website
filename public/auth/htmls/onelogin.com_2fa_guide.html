How to setup 2FA for OneLogin account?

To setup 2FA for your OneLogin account with our Authenticator App, you need to follow the next steps:

  1. Log in to your account on the OneLogin website, click your profile photo on the top right corner, then select "Settings".

  2. From the sidebar, choose "Authentication".

  3. Scroll down and click "Enable two-factor authentication".

  4. You will be taken to the "Setup authenticator app" screen where a QR code will be displayed. Please keep this popup open to complete the setup.

  5. Open our Authenticator App, click the + button at the bottom right of the screen, then hold your device near the computer screen to scan the QR code.

  6. After successfully scanning, a 6-digit code will be shown at the bottom of the screen. Please note that this code refreshes every 30 seconds.

  7. Return to the OneLogin webpage, enter the 6-digit code displayed in step 6, and then click "Continue".

  8. Remember to click "Save" in the Authenticator App to save your account, or you may lose access to your account!

  9. Now, you have successfully setup 2FA for your OneLogin account. From now on, when you attempt to sign in to your OneLogin account, you will need to use our Authenticator App.
