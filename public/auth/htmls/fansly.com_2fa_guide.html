How to setup 2FA for Fansly account?

To setup 2FA for your Fansly account with our Authenticator App, you need to follow the next steps:

  1. Log in to your account on the Fansly website, then navigate to Account Settings in the site menu.

  2. Click on Setup 2-Factor Authentication.

  3. You’ll see a QR code—keep this page open to complete the setup.

  4. Open our Authenticator App, click the + button at the bottom right of the screen, then hold your device near the computer screen to scan the QR code.

  5. After successfully scanning, a 6-digit code will be shown at the bottom of the screen. Please note that this code refreshes every 30 seconds.

  6. Return to the Fansly webpage, and enter the 6-digit code displayed in step 5, then click "Submit".

  7. Remember to click "Save" in the Authenticator App to save your account, or you may lose access to your account!

  8. Now, you have successfully setup 2FA for your Fansly account. From now on, when you attempt to sign in to your Fansly account, you will need to use our Authenticator App.
