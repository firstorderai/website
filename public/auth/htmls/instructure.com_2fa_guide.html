How to setup 2FA for Canvas account?

To setup 2FA for your Canvas account with our Authenticator App, you need to follow the next steps:

  1. Log in to your account on the Canvas website. In the Global Navigation, click the Account link, then select Settings.

  2. Click the Set Up Multi-Factor Authentication button.

  3. Select Verify via Authenticator App. A QR code containing the secret key will be displayed. Please keep this page open to complete the setup.

  4. Open our Authenticator App, click the + button at the bottom right of the screen, then hold your device near the computer screen to scan the QR code.

  5. After successfully scanning, a 6-digit code will be shown at the bottom of the screen. Please note that this code refreshes every 30 seconds.

  6. Return to the Canvas webpage, enter the 6-digit code displayed in step 5 into the Verification Code field, and then click Verify.

  7. Remember to click "Save" in the Authenticator App to save your account, or you may lose access to your account!
