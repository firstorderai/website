How to setup 2FA for VRChat account?

To setup 2FA for your VRChat account with our Authenticator App, you need to follow the next steps:

  1. In a web browser, go to the VRChat website and log in to your account.

  2. Once you've logged in, click on the "Edit Profile" button at the top left.

  3. Scroll down to the "Two-Factor Authentication" section, and click "Enable".

  4. Click "Next >" to go to the next step.

  5. In this step, a QR code containing the secret key is displayed. Next, you need to use our Authenticator App to scan this QR code. Please keep this popup open to complete the setup.

  6. Open our Authenticator App, click the + button at the bottom right of the screen, then hold your device near the computer screen to scan the QR code.

  7. After successfully scanning, a 6-digit code will be shown at the bottom of the screen. Please note that this code refreshes every 30 seconds.

  8. Return to the VRChat website, click "Next >".

  9. Enter the 6-digit code displayed in step 7, and then click on the "Verify" button.

  10. Remember to click "Save" in the Authenticator App to save your account, or you may lose access to your account!

  11. Now, you have successfully setup 2FA for your VRChat account. From now on, when you attempt to sign in to your VRChat account, you will need to use our Authenticator App.
