How to setup 2FA for Plesk account?

To setup 2FA for your Plesk account with our Authenticator App, you need to follow the next steps:

  1. Log in to your account on the Plesk website by visiting https://plesk.snel.com:8443/.

  2. Navigate to the "Multi-factor Authentication" settings in your Plesk account.

  3. Select "Enable Multi-factor Authentication".

  4. A QR code containing the secret key will be displayed. Next, you need to use our Authenticator App to scan this QR code. Please keep this popup open to complete the setup.

  5. Open our Authenticator App, click the + button at the bottom right of the screen, then hold your device near the computer screen to scan the QR code.

  6. After successfully scanning, a 6-digit code will be shown at the bottom of the screen. Please note that this code refreshes every 30 seconds.

  7. Return to the Plesk webpage, enter the 6-digit code displayed in step 6, and then click "Next".

  8. Enable the following options:

    • "Remember Device" functionality
    • Pre-select "Remember Device" checkbox
    • Remember device in days "30"

  9. Remember to click "Save" in the Authenticator App to save your account, or you may lose access to your account!

  10. Click "OK" to save the changes and activate the Two-factor authentication for your Plesk account.
