How to setup 2FA for Uphold account?

To setup 2FA for your Uphold account with our Authenticator App, you need to follow the next steps:

  1. Log in to your account on the Uphold website, click the "More (...)" button, then select "Settings".

  2. Click "Security".

  3. Click "Enable 2-factor authentication".

  4. Select "Time-based One-Time Password (TOTP)".

  5. In this step, a QR code containing the secret key is displayed. Next, you need to use our Authenticator App to scan this QR code. Please keep this popup open to complete the setup.

  6. Open our Authenticator App, click the + button at the bottom right of the screen, then hold your device near the computer screen to scan the QR code.

  7. After successfully scanning, a 6-digit code will be shown at the bottom of the screen. Please note that this code refreshes every 30 seconds.

  8. Return to the Uphold webpage, enter the 6-digit code displayed in step 7, and then click "Confirm".

  9. Remember to click "Save" in the Authenticator App to save your account, or you may lose access to your account!

  10. Now, you have successfully setup 2FA for your Uphold account. From now on, when you attempt to sign in to your Uphold account, you will need to use our Authenticator App.
