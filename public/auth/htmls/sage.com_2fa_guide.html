How to setup 2FA for Sage account?

To setup 2FA for your Sage account with our Authenticator App, you need to follow the next steps:

  1. Log in to the Account management area at account.sso.sage.com.

  2. Click 2-factor authentication and then click Send email.

  3. You'll receive an email to enroll in 2FA. Use the link in the email to set up 2FA on your account.

  4. Choose Authenticator App as your preferred method of authentication.

  5. Open your Authenticator App and choose the option to add a new account.

  6. Select the Scan a QR code option.

  7. Use your Authenticator App to scan the QR code in the 2FA setup window.

  8. In this step, a QR code containing the secret key is displayed. Next, you need to use our Authenticator App to scan this QR code. Please keep this popup open to complete the setup.

  9. Open our Authenticator App, click the + button at the bottom right of the screen, then hold your device near the computer screen to scan the QR code.

  10. After successfully scanning, a 6-digit code will be shown at the bottom of the screen. Please note that this code refreshes every 30 seconds.

  11. Return to the Sage webpage, enter the 6-digit code displayed in step 10, and then click Continue.

  12. Select the I have saved my recovery code check box, then click Continue.

  13. Remember to click "Save" in the Authenticator App to save your account, or you may lose access to your account!
