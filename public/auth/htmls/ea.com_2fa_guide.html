How to setup 2FA for Electronic Arts (Origin) account?

To setup 2FA for your Electronic Arts (Origin) account with our Authenticator App, you need to follow the next steps:

  1. Log in to your account on the Electronic Arts (Origin) website, click your avatar on the right top corner, then select "Account settings".

  2. Click "Security" in the left-hand menu.

  3. Scroll down to the "Login Verification" section, then click "Turn On".

  4. Verify your Electronic Arts (Origin) password, then click "Submit".

  5. Select "App Authenticator", then click "Continue".

  6. In this step, a QR code containing the secret key is displayed. Next, you need to use our Authenticator App to scan this QR code. Please keep this popup open to complete the setup.

  7. Open our Authenticator App, click the + button at the bottom right of the screen, then hold your device near the computer screen to scan the QR code.

  8. After successfully scanning, a 6-digit code will be shown at the bottom of the screen. Please note that this code refreshes every 30 seconds.

  9. Return to the Electronic Arts (Origin) webpage, enter the 6-digit code displayed in step 8, and then click "Submit".

  10. Remember to click "Save" in the Authenticator App to save your account, or you may lose access to your account!

  11. Now, you have successfully setup 2FA for your Electronic Arts (Origin) account. From now on, when you attempt to sign in to your Electronic Arts (Origin) account, you will need to use our Authenticator App.
