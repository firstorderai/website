How to setup 2FA for Canada Revenue Agency account?

To setup 2FA for your Canada Revenue Agency account with our Authenticator App, you need to follow the next steps:

  1. Sign in or create an account using GCKey.

  2. Choose a two-factor authentication method: Select "Use your smartphone or tablet".

  3. Scan the QR code or enter the secret key: A QR code containing the secret key will be displayed. Please keep this page open to complete the setup.

  4. Open our Authenticator App: Click the + button at the bottom right of the screen, then hold your device near the computer screen to scan the QR code.

  5. Enter the 6-digit passcode: After successfully scanning, a 6-digit code will be shown at the bottom of the screen. Please note that this code refreshes every 30 seconds.

  6. Enter the 6-digit passcode on the registration page: Return to the Canada Revenue Agency webpage, enter the 6-digit code displayed in step 5, and then click "Continue".

  7. Save your recovery codes: Once you confirm that you have safely stored your recovery codes, your registration is complete.

  8. Save your account in the Authenticator App: Remember to click "Save" in the Authenticator App to save your account, or you may lose access to your account!

  9. Add recovery codes to our app: If you have saved your recovery codes, you can add them to our Authenticator App for easy access in case you lose your device.
