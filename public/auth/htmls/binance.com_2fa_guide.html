How to setup 2FA for Binance account?

To setup 2FA for your Binance account with our Authenticator App, you need to follow the next steps:

  1. Log into your Binance account and mouse over the [Profile] icon. Click [Account].

  2. Go to [Security]. Click [Manage] next to [Authenticator App].

  3. Click [Enable Authenticator App].

  4. You’ll see a QR code and a setup key. Do not click [Next] before the Authenticator App setup is complete.

  5. Open our Authenticator App, click the + button at the bottom right of the screen, then hold your device near the computer screen to scan the QR code.

  6. After successfully scanning, a 6-digit code will be shown at the bottom of the screen. Please note that this code refreshes every 30 seconds.

  7. Return to the Binance webpage, click [Next].

  8. Enter the 6-digit code displayed in step 6, and then click [Next].

  9. Remember to click "Save" in the Authenticator App to save your account, or you may lose access to your account!

  10. You have successfully enabled 2FA for your Binance account.
