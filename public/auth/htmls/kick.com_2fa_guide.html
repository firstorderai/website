How to setup 2FA for Kick account?

To setup 2FA for your Kick account with our Authenticator App, you need to follow the next steps:

  1. Go to your channel security page: kick.com.

  2. Click the "Enable 2FA" button.

  3. Scan the QR code or type in the code to your Authenticator App.

  4. Open our Authenticator App, click the + button at the bottom right of the screen, then hold your device near the computer screen to scan the QR code.

  5. After successfully scanning, a 6-digit code will be shown at the bottom of the screen. Please note that this code refreshes every 30 seconds.

  6. Return to the Kick webpage, click "Continue Setup".

  7. Enter the 6-digit code displayed in step 5, and then click "Continue Setup".

  8. Remember to click "Save" in the Authenticator App to save your account, or you may lose access to your account!
