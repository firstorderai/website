How to setup 2FA for Nexon account?

To setup 2FA for your Nexon account with our Authenticator App, you need to follow the next steps:

  1. Log in to your Nexon account using your email address.

  2. From your account menu, click "Account Settings".

  3. Select "Password & Security".

  4. From the Two-Factor Authentication (2FA) promo window, under "Enable Authenticator App", click "Enable".

  5. From the Account Settings page, select "Password & Security". Under Security, find Authenticator App, and then click "Enable".

  6. To confirm your identity, a verification code is sent to either your email address or mobile phone. Enter the verification code. The Enable Authenticator window is displayed.

  7. A QR code containing the secret key is displayed. Next, you need to use our Authenticator App to scan this QR code. Please keep this popup open to complete the setup.

  8. Open our Authenticator App, click the + button at the bottom right of the screen, then hold your device near the computer screen to scan the QR code.

  9. After successfully scanning, a 6-digit code will be shown at the bottom of the screen. Please note that this code refreshes every 30 seconds.

  10. Return to the Nexon webpage, enter the 6-digit code displayed in step 9, and then click "Enable". An Authenticator Successfully Enabled message appears.

  11. Remember to click "Save" in the Authenticator App to save your account, or you may lose access to your account!

  12. Click "Generate Backup Codes" to create 10, 8-digit backup codes now (recommended). We encourage you to add these backup codes to our app for safekeeping.
