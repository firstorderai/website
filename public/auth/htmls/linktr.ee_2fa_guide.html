How to setup 2FA for Linktree account?

To setup 2FA for your Linktree account with our Authenticator App, you need to follow the next steps:

  1. Log in to your account on the Linktree website, and head to the My Account page in your Linktree Admin.

  2. Scroll down to the Security and privacy section.

  3. Select Authenticator App as your preferred MFA option.

  4. Tap Add and follow the app instructions to link it to your account.

  5. In this step, a QR code containing the secret key is displayed. Next, you need to use our Authenticator App to scan this QR code. Please keep this popup open to complete the setup.

  6. Open our Authenticator App, click the + button at the bottom right of the screen, then hold your device near the computer screen to scan the QR code.

  7. After successfully scanning, a 6-digit code will be shown at the bottom of the screen. Please note that this code refreshes every 30 seconds.

  8. Return to the Linktree webpage, and enter the 6-digit code displayed in step 7, then click "Next".

  9. Remember to click "Save" in the Authenticator App to save your account, or you may lose access to your account!

  10. Now, you have successfully setup 2FA for your Linktree account. From now on, when you attempt to sign in to your Linktree account, you will need to use our Authenticator App.
