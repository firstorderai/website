How to setup 2FA for Wordfence Central account?

To setup 2FA for your Wordfence Central account with our Authenticator App, you need to follow the next steps:

  1. Log in to your account on the Wordfence Central website.

  2. Navigate to the Account page.

  3. Click "Enable with Authenticator App".

  4. A window will pop up with a QR code. Keep this window open to complete the setup.

  5. Open our Authenticator App, click the + button at the bottom right of the screen, then hold your device near the computer screen to scan the QR code.

  6. After successfully scanning, a 6-digit code will be shown at the bottom of the screen. Please note that this code refreshes every 30 seconds.

  7. Return to the Wordfence Central webpage, and enter the 6-digit code displayed in step 6.

  8. Remember to click "Save" in the Authenticator App to save your account, or you may lose access to your account!

  9. Now, you have successfully setup 2FA for your Wordfence Central account. From now on, when you attempt to sign in to your Wordfence Central account, you will need to use our Authenticator App.
