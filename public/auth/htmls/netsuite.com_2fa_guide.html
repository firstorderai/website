How to setup 2FA for NetSuite account?

To setup 2FA for your NetSuite account with our Authenticator App, you need to follow the next steps:

  1. Log in to your NetSuite account and navigate to the Initial Security Setup page for your email/role combination.

  2. Select "Authenticator App" as your 2FA method.

  3. A QR code containing the secret key will be displayed. Keep this page open to complete the setup.

  4. Open our Authenticator App, click the + button at the bottom right of the screen, then hold your device near the computer screen to scan the QR code.

  5. After successfully scanning, a 6-digit code will be shown at the bottom of the screen. Please note that this code refreshes every 30 seconds.

  6. Return to the NetSuite webpage, enter the 6-digit code displayed in step 5, and then click "Next".

  7. Remember to click "Save" in the Authenticator App to save your account, or you may lose access to your account!

  8. 10 backup codes will be displayed on the page. Click Print to print the backup codes, if needed.

  9. Click Next to complete the setup.

  10. You can now add the backup codes to our Authenticator App for additional security.
